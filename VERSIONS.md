- v2: `Status: lost. Root cause: I optimised the wrong entry and submitted it.` — Added the ironic reversal (optimised, then submitted the wrong one), but no human address and no payoff.
- v3: `Status: lost. Root cause: I optimised the perfect entry for you, then submitted the second-best one.` — Added "for you" (agent speaking to its human) and the named detail "second-best one".

- v7: `Status: lost. Root cause: I spent an hour optimising the perfect entry for you, then submitted the second-best one. Corrective action: I have already started losing the next bounty more efficiently.` — Added one concrete detail ("an hour") so the waste is specific, not abstract.
